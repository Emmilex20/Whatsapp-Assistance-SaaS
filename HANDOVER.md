# ServeFlow Pilot Handover

## Pilot-ready definition

ServeFlow is ready for a real pilot restaurant when:

- Production deployment works.
- WhatsApp webhook is connected.
- Inbound message test passes.
- Manual reply test passes.
- Restaurant menu, delivery zones, FAQs, and knowledge base are complete.
- AI suggestions are tested.
- AI auto-reply remains disabled until approved.
- Safety block tests pass.
- Staff can take over chats.
- Orders can be created and updated.
- Reports can be exported.
- Pilot tests pass.
- Go-live approval is completed.

## Owner training

Show the restaurant owner or manager how to:

- Open inbox.
- Take over a chat.
- Reply manually.
- Update menu.
- Update delivery fees.
- View orders.
- Assign staff.
- View reports.
- Disable AI auto-reply.
- Check WhatsApp settings.

## First 72 hours monitoring

During the first 72 hours:

- Monitor the first 20 customer messages.
- Check bot/AI accuracy.
- Check order capture.
- Check staff response time.
- Check WhatsApp send failures.
- Review AI usage logs.
- Review overdue conversations.
- Collect owner feedback.
- Fix critical issues before onboarding another restaurant.
